# Placement Experience Poster
As Part of my Placement Year in the Sussex Attention Lab, I had to produce a poster. I chose to have my poster as a culmination of my Placement Experiences.

This experience has inspired me to pursue a post-grad research career in psychophysiology, neuroimaging, and cognitive neuroscience. 

I look forward to continuing my research during my summer project and dissertation.
